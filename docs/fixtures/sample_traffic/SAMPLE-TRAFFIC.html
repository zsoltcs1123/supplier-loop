Supplier-Sim — sample traffic (throwaway world, not your assignment)

20 inbound supplier emails across four practice rounds from a throwaway token and seed, plus three escalation → approver exchanges. Every reply style you will meet is here: inline text, pasted tables, PDF quotes (with their extracted text layer) and photos of screens.

The numbers, the suppliers' behaviour and the mix of issues in YOUR rounds will differ — this world is not yours. Use these samples only to calibrate parsers and to set expectations about tone, formats and timing. The raw files are in emails/ (JSON + attachments + index.csv) so you can point a parser at them.
