# Price history — last quarter's accepted quotes (EXAMPLE)

This file shows the FORMAT of the price history. The real history
for YOUR round comes from the `get_price_history()` MCP tool — call
it; the numbers below belong to a throwaway example round, not to
your assignment.

The history lists the unit price each supplier last charged us per
material. Use it as your baseline when judging whether a new
quote's pricing has drifted: quotes are expected to sit close to
last quarter's accepted price, and your RFQ's
`target_price_ceiling_pct` tells you how much of a rise is
tolerable before it needs a human call.
